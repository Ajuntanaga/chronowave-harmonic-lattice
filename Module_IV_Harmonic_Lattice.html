Any eigenbasis on the major-thirds clock

The bridge between the music theory and the physics — now fully general. Each chromatic interval is a 30° step; toggle any set of intervals to define your eigenbasis. The qubit kets resize to the basis you choose, the trig identity-distances light up wherever your scale lands on them, and Measure collapses the superposition to a single sounding state.

The original scale — 1 2 ♭3 3 ♭5 ♭6 ♭7 (1′) — remains the special case where every eigenstate lands exactly on a trigonometric identity. Four zero states |0⟩ sit a half-step prior to each augmented-triad anchor — before 1, 3, ♭6, and 1′ — a four-fold degenerate ground manifold on the M3 skeleton. Measurement can collapse onto a zero: the outcome is a rest. Toggle intervals to build any basis; the register below shows every ket, and selecting an identity node sketches its polar curve with its exact value at the 30° semitone unit.

The chord chips above are generated from the active scale — tertian stacks by scale step on every degree — so each basis carries its own harmony.
